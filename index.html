Your Move

The world changes. Your move.

It’s 1777, and a courier just handed you a letter naming a spy inside your own regiment. You recognize the handwriting — it isn’t the traitor’s. Now you have to decide whether to expose the letter as a forgery, or let an innocent man hang for it.

It’s 1986 in Miami. The jewelry exchange on Ocean Drive was the biggest score of your career, and you swore under oath you were home all night. Now a detective slides a photograph across the table — you, in the vault’s service alley, timestamped four minutes before the alarm tripped. What do you say?

It’s 2028, three days after coordinated high-altitude EMP strikes took down the country’s grid. Your building’s old diesel generator — no electronic ignition, nothing to fry — is one of the few things still running, and it has six hours of fuel left to power either the water pump or the medical fridge holding a neighbor’s insulin. Forty people are asking which one you’ll choose. You’re not reading about it. You’re the one who has to decide.

YOUR MOVE — 90 SECONDS

We need a decision. Now.

No signup. One scenario, real information, one decision — and a look at what the world does with it. This is one of hundreds of moments like it.

The reservoir valve fails in 15 minutes, no matter what you do. Riverside General has 40 patients on ventilators and backup power that’s already flickering. Eastgate Apartments has 1,200 residents in a heat wave with no working AC. You can only protect one district’s water pressure — you’re choosing who loses it first, and for how long.

Not a movie. Not a quiz. Not a game with four buttons.

Your Move puts you inside a living situation with people, information, pressure, limited resources, hidden motives, and consequences. The world responds to what you actually do.

Nothing lands in isolation. The ally you saved may owe you later — or resent how it happened. The lead you ignored can surface again, worse. Ripples move through history, crime, and everything between: some you’ll see coming, some you won’t.

HOW IT WORKS

The world doesn’t wait for you to pick A, B, or C.

Your Move is powered by an AI-driven world engine that tracks what you do, what you ignore, who you trust, what you ask, and how you respond under pressure. The world changes around those decisions — sometimes immediately, sometimes much later.

It remembers how you play.

With memory enabled, Your Move carries what it learns from one decision into the next.

Ask experts early, every time? The next world may make expertise harder to find.
Move fast on limited information? Speed helps you here — and costs you elsewhere.
Avoid uncertain social situations? The world can make that blind spot matter.

You control difficulty. It controls variation.

DIFFICULTY

Decide how hard you want the world to push back.

Harder worlds don’t cheat. They give you less certainty, tighter resources, more conflicting information, and less room for error.

StoryGuided pacing, generous margin for error — built for a first world or a slower evening.
StandardBalanced stakes and information — the world pushes back, but rarely without warning.
VeteranTighter resources and margins — mistakes cost you, and there’s rarely a clean second chance.
ExpertLittle certainty about anything — sources conflict, and you rarely know what you don’t know.
BrutalNo safety net. The world plays for keeps, and it won’t slow down to let you catch up.
